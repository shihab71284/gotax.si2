Not legal or tax advice.

GoTax.SI provides AI-assisted explanations and document preparation support. It is not a law firm or a government agency and is not affiliated with the IRS. Information is general and not legal or tax advice.

1. No professional relationship from AI output

Using the Notice Decoder, reading an explanation or downloading a PDF does not create an attorney–client, accountant–client or preparer–taxpayer relationship. A professional relationship with a licensed preparer exists only once you engage our CPA/EA partner for return preparation or human review, and it is limited to that engagement.

2. AI can be wrong

Explanations are generated from the text we can read from your document. OCR of a skewed or low-quality photo can misread digits; the model can misclassify a notice. That is why we show a confidence score, quote the sentence each field came from, display “Not found, please check the original notice.” when a field is missing, and never invent amounts or deadlines. Always verify against the original letter before you act, pay or respond.

3. Deadlines are yours to protect

Deadlines shown are read from your document or from typical values for that notice type, clearly labelled as such. Missing a deadline can have serious consequences, including loss of appeal rights, penalties, interest, levies or liens. If a deadline is close or the notice mentions a levy, lien, audit or identity theft, request a human review or contact a licensed professional immediately.

4. Translations

Translations are machine-assisted and provided for convenience. The English version of every explanation, page and legal document controls in case of any difference. Tax terms do not always map cleanly between languages — if something is unclear, use the English pane or ask us.

5. We do not contact the IRS for you

Draft response letters are prepared for you to review, sign and send yourself, or to hand to a licensed professional. Nothing is transmitted to the IRS automatically by this website.

6. Not a government service

IRS notice codes (CP2000, CP14, CP11, CP501, CP504, LTR3219C, LTR5071C, LTR569C and others) are used only to identify documents. Free official help is available from the IRS at irs.gov, and low-income taxpayers may qualify for free help through the IRS Taxpayer Advocate Service, VITA or TCE programmes.

7. No guarantee of outcome

We cannot guarantee that a notice will be resolved, that an amount will be reduced, that a refund will be issued, or that a return will be accepted without adjustment. Outcomes depend on the facts, the law and the tax authority.

8. Licensed review

Prepared returns are reviewed and signed by a licensed US CPA or EA partner before filing. The preparer's name and PTIN are displayed once assigned. Human review of a notice is performed by a licensed professional who provides their own written assessment.

9. Jurisdictional limits

The service relates to US federal tax matters (and, where offered, US state returns). It does not cover the tax law of other countries, and it is not immigration, legal or financial-planning advice.

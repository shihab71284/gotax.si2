About GoTax.SI

Every year, millions of taxpayers receive cryptic IRS notices such as CP2000, CP14, CP504, and Letter 3219C. GoTax.SI was created to give taxpayers immediate clarity in their own language before costly deadlines expire.

Our Core Principles

  • 12-Language Accessibility: Side-by-side plain-language explanations in English, Spanish, Hindi, Bangla, Arabic, Swahili, German, Korean, Tagalog, French, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.
  • Zero-Knowledge Client-Side Privacy: Sensitive SSN/ITIN and bank numbers are masked directly in your browser before analysis.
  • Licensed Professional Oversight: Optional return preparation and complex notice representation are reviewed and signed by licensed US CPA/EA partners.

Internal Revenue Service
Letter 5071C — Please provide tax return information so we can verify your
identity

Date: 03/28/2025
Tax year: 2024

Taxpayer: SAMPLE PERSON
Social security number: 123-45-6789

We need to verify your identity before we can finish processing your 2024 tax
return. We received your return but we cannot process it until you complete
identity verification. Your refund will not be issued until this is done.

WHY YOU RECEIVED THIS LETTER
Our filters identified characteristics that may indicate identity theft, so we
must confirm that you — and not someone else — filed this return.

WHAT YOU NEED TO DO — choose one
- Verify online at irs.gov/identityverify. You will need your filed return, a
  personal account in your name (such as a mobile phone or credit card), and a
  government-issued photo identification such as a driver licence or passport.
- Or call us at 1-800-738-1039 (not the general IRS line) Monday through
  Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. local time.
- If you cannot verify online or by phone, make an appointment at a Taxpayer
  Assistance Center by calling 1-844-545-5640.

Please complete verification within 30 days of this letter, by 04/27/2025.
Until you verify, your return is not processed and your refund is delayed.

DID YOU NOT FILE THIS RETURN?
If you did not file the return described in this letter, tell us immediately.
Complete Form 14039, Identity Theft Affidavit, and report the theft at
identitytheft.gov. Consider placing a fraud alert or credit freeze with the
three credit bureaus and request an Identity Protection PIN (IP PIN) at
irs.gov/ippin.

Never verify your identity through a link in an unexpected email or text
message. Always go to irs.gov directly.

2. How auto-deletion works

Every stored file is written with a deleteAt timestamp derived from the retention setting in force at upload time. A scheduled job deletes expired files and their OCR output, then records an entry in the audit log. Object storage also has a lifecycle rule as a second line of defence, and backups age out on their own cycle — [backup cycle, e.g. 30 days — placeholder]. Deletion from backups is complete no later than [35 days — placeholder] after the primary deletion.

3. Deleting your data yourself

  • One file: dashboard → Documents → delete. Immediate.
  • One explanation: dashboard → Explanations → delete. Immediate.
  • Everything: dashboard → Data & privacy → “Delete all my data”. You type DELETE to confirm. This removes documents, OCR text, explanations and audit entries tied to your account, and signs you out. Payment records and consents are retained as required by law.
  • Shorten your window: dashboard → Data & privacy → set retention to 7, 30, 60, 90, 180 or 365 days.

4. Data we must keep

If a licensed preparer filed a return for you, tax record-keeping rules require the return, the supporting documents and the signed consents to be kept for the statutory period. We will tell you exactly what is retained and why when you ask, and we will keep only that.

5. Requests by email

Email privacy@gotax.si with the subject “deletion request”. We verify your identity by the email address on the account, then act within [30 days — placeholder]. You receive a confirmation with the deletion reference.

6. What is never stored

  • SSNs, ITINs and bank account numbers in logs — they are masked before processing and only masked values are stored.
  • Full document contents in logs or analytics events.
  • Card numbers anywhere — cards are tokenised by the payment provider.
  • Bitcoin private keys — payments are handled by the processor.

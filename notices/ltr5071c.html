IRS notice explained · LTR5071C

Identity verification required before your refund is released

The IRS needs you to verify your identity before it can process your return or release your refund.

What this notice is

A 5071C (or similar letter such as CP01B / 5747C) means the IRS flagged your return for potential identity theft and needs you to confirm that you filed it.

  • Until you verify, the refund is frozen.
  • If you did NOT file the return, treat this as confirmed identity theft and report it immediately.

What the IRS is asking

Complete identity verification using the method the letter specifies — online at the IRS ID Verify site or by phone at the number in the letter.

  • You will need your filing, a personal account (credit card in your name, phone, email), and identity documents such as a driver's licence or passport.
  • Never verify through a link in an unsolicited email or text — go directly to irs.gov.

Deadline and what happens if you miss it

The letter states a deadline; if you miss it your return is not processed and the refund is delayed further.

  • Verification usually takes a few minutes; refunds are typically released within 6–9 weeks after verification.

Your options (pay, agree, disagree, request more time)

  • I FILED IT — verify identity online or by phone.
  • I DID NOT FILE IT — report identity theft: Form 14039, identitytheft.gov, and consider freezing your credit.
  • NO ONLINE ACCESS — call the number in the letter or visit a Taxpayer Assistance Center by appointment.

Documents you may need

  • Government photo ID, your filed return, and any prior-year refund or account information the IRS asks for.

Recommended next steps

  • 1. Verify through irs.gov or the phone number printed in the letter only.
  • 2. Keep the confirmation number.
  • 3. If you did not file the return, submit Form 14039 and place fraud alerts with the credit bureaus.
  • 4. Monitor your IRS online account for changes.

Questions to ask a tax professional

  • Did someone else file a return using my SSN/ITIN?
  • Should I file Form 14039 and an identity-theft PIN (IP PIN) request?
  • How long will my refund be delayed after verification?
  • Should I freeze my credit files?

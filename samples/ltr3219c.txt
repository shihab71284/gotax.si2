YOUR RIGHT TO A HEARING
You have the right to request a Collection Due Process (CDP) hearing with the
IRS Independent Office of Appeals. To request a hearing, you must complete and
mail Form 12153, Request for a Collection Due Process or Equivalent Hearing,
so that it is postmarked within 30 days of the date of this notice.

- Pay the balance in full immediately.
- Call us to set up an instalment agreement or submit an offer in compromise.

  hearing and raise spousal defences.
- If paying would prevent you from meeting necessary living expenses, ask
  about currently-not-collectible status and contact the Taxpayer Advocate
  Service.

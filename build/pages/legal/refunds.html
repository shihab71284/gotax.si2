2. How to request a refund

  1. Email support@gotax.si or use the contact form, choosing the payment topic.
  2. Include your receipt number (RCT-…) or invoice number (INV-…) and, for Bitcoin payments, the transaction ID.
  3. We acknowledge within [1 business day — placeholder] and decide within [5 business days — placeholder].
  4. Approved refunds are recorded in the admin console and shown on your dashboard with a refund reference.

3. Bitcoin payments and volatility

The USD price is fixed. The BTC amount is calculated from the processor's live exchange rate when the invoice is created and locked for the invoice period (default 15 minutes). If the invoice expires, the rate is no longer valid and a new invoice at the current rate is issued — this is not a refund event. Refunds of Bitcoin payments are returned in BTC at the rate applied at the time of the refund, or in USD by bank transfer where you prefer; network fees are deducted and shown on the refund record. Lightning payments are refunded on-chain to an address you provide, since Lightning invoices cannot be paid back to the same route.

4. Card and bank payments

Refunds are returned to the original payment method by the processor. Timing depends on your bank, typically [5–10 business days — placeholder].

5. Chargebacks

Please talk to us first — we resolve payment problems quickly. A chargeback on a delivered service may result in the service being suspended and the entitlement revoked while the dispute is investigated.

6. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits your statutory rights, including any right of withdrawal that applies where you live, or our liability for fraud or gross negligence. Where consumer law gives you a longer period or stronger rights, those apply.

7. Records

Every refund is recorded with a reference number, the invoice it relates to, the amount, the reason, who approved it and a timestamp. Refund records appear on your dashboard and in the admin console.
